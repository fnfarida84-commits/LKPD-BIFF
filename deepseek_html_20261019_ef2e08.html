Relationship between Frequency & Wavelength
Investigation question: How does changing frequency affect wavelength when string conditions and amplitude are kept constant?

Work steps

  • 1Set the amplitude to 1.00 cm and keep the string conditions constant throughout the experiment.
  • 2Set the frequency to 0.50 Hz. Observe the distance between two consecutive wave crests (wavelength).
  • 3Increase the frequency to 1.00 Hz without changing amplitude and string conditions. Observe again the distance between consecutive crests.
